The left pane is the full source file. Paragraphs carry production markup (\tagStructPara{}\paraid{...}\NeoParStart{..}...\tagStructParaEnd{}%). Typing inside a marked paragraph re-breaks it through the persistent LuaTeX engine (~1–3 ms) and overlays it (dark red) at its cached page position; edits anywhere else (preamble, headings, floats) go to the background path. After ~1.2 s idle the full edited source recompiles for real and the page cache converges. Glyph positions are engine-exact; outlines are a browser-font stand-in.
