Right side: the real document pages — every glyph position comes from LuaTeX's shipout node structures (title, abstract, two-column body, floats, footnotes). Typing re-breaks the edited paragraph through the persistent engine in ~1–3 ms and overlays it (dark red) at its cached page position. After ~1.5 s idle, a real full compile runs in the background and the page cache converges — page breaks, column balance and positions update, and the overlay merges back to black. Glyph outlines are a browser-font stand-in; positions are engine-exact.
