Every keystroke round-trips through a persistent lualatex process: the edited paragraph is re-broken by Knuth–Plass with its captured context (measure, font, microtype, language) and re-rendered from the engine's node structures. Glyph positions are engine-exact scaled points; glyph outlines use your browser's serif font as a stand-in. Unedited paragraphs render from the full compile's cached reference — the background- convergence overlay pattern.
