\title{Paragraph-Local Recompilation in Production Journal Classes}
\author[1]{A. Author}
\author[1]{B. Author}
\address[1]{Institute for Typesetting Research, Chennai, India}
\begin{abstract}
We evaluate per-paragraph real-time recompilation against production
journal templates and verify that isolated recompilation reproduces the
reference line breaks exactly when the layout context is reinjected.
\end{abstract}
